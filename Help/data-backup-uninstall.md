# Data, Backup, Import/Export, and Uninstall

This topic covers how to move, restore, or remove your app data safely.

## Backup and migration

- Keep these files from your app folder:
  - `settings.json`
  - `feeds.json`
- Restore by placing them next to `MyNewsFeeder.exe`.

## Import and export feeds

- Open `Manage Feeds`.
- Use `Import` / `Export` with:
  - `OPML` (best for moving feeds between different readers)
  - `JSON` (best for My News Feeder to My News Feeder migration)

## Uninstall (full cleanup)

1. Close app and tray icon.
2. Delete the app folder.
3. Optional cleanup:
   - `%LocalAppData%\MyNewsFeeder`
   - `%TEMP%\MyNewsFeeder`
   - Start menu shortcuts for My News Feeder
