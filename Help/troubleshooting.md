# Troubleshooting

## Slow refresh spikes

- Usually caused by a few slow feeds.
- Increase refresh interval or disable problematic feeds.
- Keep Live Refresh at 60s for stable operation.

## Reader Mode incomplete content

- Some websites block extraction behind cookie/consent walls.
- You can use **Show Content** to read the page inside the app instead of opening your default external browser.
- Or use **Open Original** if reader extraction is limited.(will open your default external browser)

## Notifications missing or not opening article

- Ensure **Desktop notifications for important items** is enabled in app **Settings**.
- Check Windows notifications are enabled for the app.
- Verify app identity/shortcut was created by launching from your normal app folder.
- Restart app once after updates if toast behavior looks inconsistent.
- On some Windows 10 systems, clicking a toast may not always open the article directly.
- Fallback: open **Latest Important Feeds** with **Ctrl+I** and open the article from there.
