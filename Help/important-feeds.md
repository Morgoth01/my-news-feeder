# Important Feeds and Notifications

Important notifications are triggered only for genuinely new important items.

## Important criteria

- Feed marked as important in Feed Manager, or
- Match against configured important keywords.

## Latest Important Feeds window

- Open it from the tray icon context menu (**Show latest important feeds**) or with **Ctrl+I**.
- Shows latest important entries.
- Supports clear all and per-item remove.
- Supports actions like Read Later and Pin.
- Persists across app restarts.
- Auto cleanup can be configured in the window: **24 hours**, **3 days**, **7 days**, or **Never**.
- The list stores up to **200 entries** maximum.

Items remain in this list until you clear them, so you do not miss important news.

## Windows 10 note

- On some Windows 10 systems, clicking a toast notification may not always open the article directly.
- Use **Latest Important Feeds** (**Ctrl+I**) as a reliable fallback.
